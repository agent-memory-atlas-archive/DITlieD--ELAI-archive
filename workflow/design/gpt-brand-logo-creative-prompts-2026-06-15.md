WHAT MUST NOT MOVE (brand law — these stay even when everything else is free):
- near-black ground, flat. No gradient, no glow, no glassmorphism, no drop shadows, no 3D, no bevel, no texture (glow is brand-HERO-only, never on a flat mark).

- must stay legible at 16px (favicon scale) and survive being collapsed to one flat color.
WHAT IS FREE (this is where the creativity goes): the concept, the geometry, letterform vs monogram vs abstract symbol vs constellation vs aperture vs glyph, the composition, and the white-vs-teal treatment per mark (a white mark with one teal accent, OR a solid teal mark on black, OR a teal outline, OR a negative-space cut — GPT's call, varied across the ten).

WHAT ELAI IS (give GPT real meaning to draw from, so the marks mean something instead of being decoration):
ELAI is a dark, precise developer instrument — a deterministic engine, not a chatty assistant. Its whole identity is TRUST and EVIDENCE: work proceeds as a chain of verified steps, each one checked, nothing taken on faith. Think of motifs like: a single verified signal in the dark, a constellation of confirmed points, a checkmark/node that proves itself, an aperture or focus, forward motion through a graph, an instrument readout, a seed of order in negative space. The brand voice is quiet, minimal, radically spacious, hairline-and-geometric — confident through restraint, never loud. The name is ELAI (E, L, A, I).
