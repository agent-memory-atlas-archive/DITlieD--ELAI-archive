This is ONE committed design, not a menu — the three buttons top-right only swap the GROUND (slate-green / lavender / deep); the palette + type + layout stay fixed so you judge the design, not a repaint. The colour is multi-tone with meaning: cyan = live, sage = done, slate = pending, mauve = a category (worker source), amber = needs-input, and the white inversion = error. That is deliberately NOT "one accent" — it is the GITS filmic palette used semantically, which is why it reads different from everything before. Type is Chakra Petch (headers) + IBM Plex Sans (UI) + IBM Plex Mono (data), no terminal-mono-everywhere. Tell me: right palette? right ground? and is the multi-tone-semantic direction the one, or do you want it pulled tighter (fewer hues) or looser (more GITS purples/ambers)?
