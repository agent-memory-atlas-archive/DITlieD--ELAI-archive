Signature 2

Signature 3

Hairline architecture

No fills anywhere — every surface is the same ground, and structure is carried by 1px lines only. DESIGN.md §3 already says "the hairline, not the lightness delta, makes the edge read"; this makes the line the whole identity. A schematic / blueprint austerity.

Signature 4

Mono-inversion gesture

Elevate the light-on-dark inversion (today only the fail chip) into a repeatable punch — the single most important element on any surface becomes an inverted block. Here it marks the failing session AND the run that needs attention. A recognizable emphatic move nobody else uses (everyone reaches for a color).
