GPT image idea prompts — logo / type / website, 3 variations each
For MANUAL use in ChatGPT "Create image" (gpt-bridge is banned after the 2026-06-12 crash; paste these yourself whenever you want previews). Built per .claude/rulebook/gpt-image-prompting.md: visual-spec form, aspect folded into the prompt, EXACT text rules. To iterate, re-paste the FULL prompt with your change folded in, never "make it better". Caveat for the TYPE prompts: image models approximate typefaces — treat those renders as vibe previews, the real font decision happens in a local HTML specimen with the actual font files.

The locked facts these prompts encode (do not let a render talk you out of them): logo = the monoline stroke wordmark E L A I (10 strokes, thin lines, wide spacing, drawn stroke-by-stroke in the real product); one teal accent on near-black; palette is 4 values; warn/fail are glyphs (triangle / X mono-inversion), never colors; galaxy is brand-only, work surfaces flat.

================ LOGO (3 variations) ================

--- L1: lockup sheet (wordmark refinements) ---

Deliverable: a wide 16:9 landscape logo lockup sheet on a single flat near-black background, design-document style.
Scene: flat very dark blue-black background, matte, no texture.
Subjects: four variations of ONE logo arranged in a 2x2 grid, each a wordmark reading "ELAI" built from thin monoline strokes (uniform stroke width, rounded line ends, geometric skeleton letters: E as three horizontal bars on a vertical, L as vertical plus base, A as two slanted strokes with a crossbar, I as a single vertical), wide letter spacing. Variation differences: stroke weight slightly lighter / slightly heavier / wider letter spacing / tighter letter spacing.
Composition: 2x2 grid, generous spacing, each variation labeled beneath with a tiny monospace caption "01" "02" "03" "04".
Lighting and mood: flat, even, no glow, no shadows; precise engineering-drawing mood.
Style: minimal brand-guidelines sheet, vector look, monoline geometry.
Text in image: EXACT, verbatim: "ELAI" appears exactly four times as the stroke wordmark in muted teal. Captions "01" "02" "03" "04" once each, small, pale gray. No other words.
Constraints: no watermark, no gradients, no glow, single teal accent on near-black only, strokes stay thin and geometric, do not turn the letters into a normal typeface.

Composition: one horizontal row of six equal tiles, small monospace number under each.
Lighting and mood: flat, even, no glow except a faint brightness on the dot elements.
Style: minimal icon design sheet, vector look, monoline.
Text in image: only the numbers "1" through "6", small, pale gray, once each. No letters except the strokes that form the glyphs, no words.
Constraints: no watermark, no gradients, single muted teal accent on near-black, every glyph must stay readable at tiny sizes, thin strokes only.
