Layered Topology — interaction study

Two ways to navigate a graph whose nodes open into deeper graphs: systems open into their code, plan steps open into their research. The navigation metaphor is borrowed from Chrome DevTools' Layers panel — a stack you descend, a tree that mirrors it, a detail pane on the side.

Offline mockup · hand-rolled SVG + vanilla JS · sample data is ELAI-flavored, not wired to live state.
