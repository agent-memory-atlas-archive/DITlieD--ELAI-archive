- Over-redaction on key-shaped benign content (DD7) — the FP corpus is the guard; err toward NOT redacting ambiguous short tokens (a redactor that mangles code is worse than a missed low-entropy token).

- secret_redacted advisory recursion — the short-circuit on the variant is mandatory.
