SYSTEM UNDER STUDY (neutral description): We are building an agent framework in which an external controller (the "harness") deterministically manages the context window of a small local model across multiple invocations. The harness (not the model) decides when to reset and what to carry; the model only generates the reasoning and proposes the distillate. The seed inspiration is the "iterative reasoning with intermediate summarization / sawtooth memory" family (e.g. InftyThink, arXiv:2503.06692). Our larger hypothesis is that this single context-management pattern generalizes across three axes -- multi-step REASONING, hierarchical PLANNING/decomposition, and iterative RESEARCH/retrieval -- and that a harness that handles context transfer cleanly between resets can extend and outperform the basic interleaved-summarization idea for small models. We want this thoroughly grounded and, equally, thoroughly challenged.
