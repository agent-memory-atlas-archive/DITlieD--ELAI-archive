## Why I-plan exists (read this before anything else)

TRACK 12 — FAULT-ISOLATION + ENFORCEMENT-PLANE (I-1..I-3). Authored 2026-06-10 from the Consolidated draft
Part I + the trust-review A3. CENTRAL GROUNDED FINDING: ELAI's enforcement runs IN-PROCESS with the thing it
restrains (run_pre_tool_use hooks share the generator's address space; the 2026 incident record — an agent
disabling its own sandbox — shows why that boundary must be structural), and parallel work shares mutable
scratch space. Track 12 splits reasoning (pure Intent->Directive transitions) from side-effect execution (a
supervisor that is the ONLY component executing Directives, gates first), gives parallel branches CoW/pooled
isolated workspaces with deterministic merge, and hardens the SSB/checkpoint write path. Mostly BUILD; the
supervisor boundary is the security upgrade every other track leans on. ADDS ID-2.

TRACK 13 — EVIDENCE-RANKED SEARCH (I-4..I-9). Authored 2026-06-10 from the Consolidated draft Part II +
review corrections. CENTRAL FINDING: nothing in the field ships tree search — what ships is wide parallelism
plus an LLM-judge selector; ELAI's verifier stack means selection can be BY RECEIPTS instead. The LLM is
demoted to a stateless hypothesis generator; the orchestrator owns the state tree; failed branches are pruned
from context (the vividness trap is real and MEASURABLE — I-5 instruments it before claiming it). The MCTS
XL is a ladder (best-of-N -> tournament -> tree, IC-6) so every rung ships value. ADDS ID-1, ID-4.

TRACK 14 — CACHE-INVARIANT ECONOMICS MIDDLEWARE (I-10..I-16). Authored 2026-06-10 from the Consolidated
draft Part III + ADECO + the rate-limit/caching dossier + review 1.3. CENTRAL FINDING: ELAI's deep local
validation loops are structurally at war with provider cache lifecycles and rate-limit accounting (TTL
eviction, TPM = input + max_tokens, sub-second buckets), and the fix is mechanical: byte-frozen prefixes,
explicit TTL tiering with a measured-economics planner, schema-bound max_tokens clamps, typed cross-model
belief-state handoffs, and deterministic (journaled) context compression. Every lever is MEASURED per config
(HD-1) and every middleware transform lands a receipt (ADDS ID-3). Composes H-1/H-2/H-4/H-9; restates none.

TRACK 15 — DETERMINISTIC GATES + ANTI-GAMING VERIFICATION (I-17..I-24). Authored 2026-06-10 from the
Consolidated draft Part IV + review 1.1. CENTRAL FINDING: the verification substrate (mutation runner +
cache, metamorphic relations, conformal calibration, RRA/attestation) is SHIPPED — what is absent is the
in-loop composition: a tri-layer veto stack with a halting bound and flake quarantine, budgeted diff-cone
mutation cheap enough to live inside the loop (sampled scores with confidence intervals; survivors become
regeneration Directives), zero-shared-context sprint contracts, progress-shaped rewards, ONE unified
calibration, per-row hash chaining with external transparency, and PRM anti-gaming canaries. Composition,
not invention. ADDS no new family beyond ID-4/ID-5 bindings.

TRACK 16 — ROUTING + BUDGETS + MEASUREMENT (I-25..I-29). Authored 2026-06-10 from the Consolidated draft
Part V + review corrections. CENTRAL FINDING: ELAI already owns the one asset routing products lack —
ground-truth verifier outcomes per (task-features, lane, cost) in its own journal — so the router trains on
receipts, budgets scale with serial depth (H-42), exploration stops when the marginal verified-pass per
dollar collapses, and the convergence curve (verified outcome rate vs compute) becomes the public instrument.
DAG credit assignment harvests DPO pairs as a byproduct. ADDS no new family (ID-5/ID-6 bind).

TRACK 17 — OPEN-WEIGHT FLYWHEEL (I-30..I-31). Authored 2026-06-10 from the Consolidated draft Part VI,
RE-SEQUENCED per both reviews (IC-7). CENTRAL FINDING: the search compute of Track 13 produces, for free,
verifier-ranked trajectories — the correct first distillation is rejection-sampling SFT (cheap, stable),
and GRPO comes LAST behind blocking guards because a self-improving loop whose evaluator it can influence is
the one failure that discredits the whole trust claim (H-plan Track 7's convergence theorem). Weights are
configs: every promotion passes the H-17/H-18/H-28/D-6 ledger gates. ADDS ID-6.

TRACK 18 — DETERMINISTIC-OVER-PROMPT HARDENING (I-32..I-40). Authored 2026-06-10 from the six research
dossiers + QAfromfable + the reviews' net-new items. CENTRAL FINDING: the 2025-26 empirical literature is
