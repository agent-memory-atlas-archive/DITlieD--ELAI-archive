- The AUX is explicit: the determinism doctrine (DOCTRINE.md) is the WHAT (a lens, a checkable property OF A FEATURE); the AUX (M1-M12 + the stance question) is the HOW (the reasoning METHOD that PRODUCES a feature). "If you only pattern-match 'determinism' you have missed the point — the point is a way of reasoning about any ELAI improvement." J-43 propagates the LENS via code gates; THIS file propagates the METHOD. You cannot regex the QUALITY of reasoning, but the ARTIFACT SHAPE the method mandates IS deterministically presence-checkable, and that is the lever: mechanize the structure (sections present + claims tagged), advise the substance (DP-3), fail-closed on missing structure (D3 applied to the reasoning artifact), falsify before trust (M9). CRITICAL (C2, the single most important fold-in): a shape gate that only checks SECTIONS EXIST is THEATER without a random-sample DEEP AUDIT of substance by a non-author — mandated shapes (ADRs, pre-mortems, WHO checklists) improve outcomes ONLY when audited, and PARTIAL compliance gives a NULL effect. So the substance-audit panel is a FIRST-CLASS component here, not an afterthought.

## Steal/build
1. EXP-DP2 (OBSERVE-ONLY, step 1): run reasoning_artifact_shape_check.py over the existing workflow/reasoning/*.md + plan files to measure (a) the shape-check FP rate and (b) the boilerplate rate — how often a section is PRESENT but EMPTY-of-substance (attack-theater, generic-falsifier). The boilerplate rate is the number that decides whether the substance-audit panel (step 4) is load-bearing or optional. No shape gate goes blocking-default-ON before its FP rate is bounded (GD-4) AND the substance-audit catch-rate is observed.
2. .claude/rules/reasoning-method.md — the artifact-shape + protocol meta-rule (generalizes planning-workflow's plan-improvement protocol to ALL reasoning artifacts, adding the M2/stance/M7/M10 presence checks it lacks). Mandates, for any reasoning/design/plan artifact: M1 grounding-before-claim (every external claim carries a source or a tag); M2 confidence calibration (every load-bearing claim tagged VERIFIED / high-confidence / training-unsearched); the STANCE question (a DECISION CLASSIFICATION — what the model decides here vs what the harness could decide instead); M4/M6 net-new-vs-existing (reinvention | extension | net-new + a named file:line seam); M7 attack-your-own-design (an ATTACK section, the strongest case against); M9 falsifier-as-step-1 (the cheapest falsifying experiment is literally step 1; nothing default-ON before its FP/over-block rate is bounded on >=3 runs); M10 honest residue (a RESIDUE section: unverified / provisional / deferred); M12 write-it-down (the reasoning chain + durable lens + handoff exist).
3. .claude/tools/reasoning_artifact_shape_check.py — the deterministic presence/structure check, sibling of doctrine_floor_check.py: the mandated sections EXIST + load-bearing claims are TAGGED. Missing ATTACK section = FAIL; untagged load-bearing claim = FAIL (D3 on the reasoning artifact). Regex/heading-aware, necessary-not-sufficient, exit-2 fail-closed on a missing/empty target, observe-only WARN first. It checks SHAPE only; QUALITY is step 4.
4. THE RANDOM-SAMPLE SUBSTANCE-AUDIT PANEL (C2, the load-bearing leg, NOT an afterthought): a recurring auditor-side protocol that randomly samples N reasoning artifacts (and DP-4 ledger rows) and DEEP-AUDITS their SUBSTANCE — is the ATTACK section a real strongest-case or boilerplate, is the falsifier real, is the decision-classification honest — performed by an auditor that is NOT the author (separate measurer from measured), tracking REMEDIATION VELOCITY (not just a section-present count), against a held-out un-gamed subset. "Partial = null" forces all-or-nothing WITHIN each sampled subset: you cannot half-enforce and expect half the benefit.
5. THE LIFECYCLE INJECTIONS the method binds (each reuses an existing seam; the rule + shape-check make them enforceable):
   - DESIGN-TIME (injection 1): a DECISION LEDGER pass added as a required spec-eval output dimension — for the design under review, enumerate every decision and sort model-class vs harness-class (the doctrine boundary); a harness-class decision handed to the model is a named DESIGN WEAKNESS in spec-eval's own grammar. (Cross-family vote lives in DP-3.)
   - PLAN-TIME (injection 2): a DOCTRINE PRE_GATE in planning-workflow plan-improvement step 4 (parallel to the WALKING-SKELETON PRE_GATE) + step 7 (parallel to OFF-AGENT PROOF) + idea-intake's per-level plan-auditor rubric — every deliverable declares DECISIONS / MODEL-or-HARNESS / off-agent DISPOSER per harness-class decision / a DOCTRINE-DEBT row for any legitimately-model decision with no oracle. A producer-only / disposer-less harness-class decision is REFUSED at decomposition.
   - DISPATCH-TIME (injection 4): the doctrine + method pointer added to agent-dispatch-preamble.md so every worker is doctrine-bound; extend the R10 integration gate with a SEAM-DISPOSAL check (a harness-class decision a worker implemented must be disposed by the harness, not silently by the model at the seam).
EXCLUDED: an LLM that SCORES reasoning quality on a gating path (D5; the substance audit is human/cross-family advisory, never an automated gating verdict); constrained-decoding the reasoning itself (HD-3); re-authoring planning-workflow's plan-improvement (extend it, never rewrite — GD-1); treating a section-present pass as a quality pass (the C2 trap this file exists to avoid).
