## Context
Why this matters: this is the SHAPE-B one-level-up residue (§0) applied to governance itself — ELAI gates the worker's output against measured objectives, but the RULEBOOK that defines those gates is enforced by a mix of frozen checkers (real teeth) and prose (trusted, untested). A prose-only doctrine clause is precisely the proxy wiring-enforcement R4 warns against: it LOOKS like enforcement and enforces nothing, and nobody knows the ratio. The LACUNA fill is to measure it: tag every clause by its actual enforcer, emit a coverage %, and make the unenforced clauses a visible compilation backlog (the ContextCov pattern — compile prose policy into deterministic checks where possible). The point is NOT to force every clause into a checker (some doctrine is irreducibly judgment — Rice/halting; those are AUDITOR-ADVISORY honestly) but to STOP MISTAKING prose for enforcement and to PRIORITIZE the high-value prose-only clauses for promotion. Mechanism: a deterministic tool reads the rulebook + the gate-manifest, joins each clause to its enforcer, emits the ledger + the coverage metric; a thin rule binds future rulebook edits to declare a clause's enforcement tag (so the ledger does not re-drift); the backlog is a PROPOSAL the user ratifies (no silent checker-build). This is K-8's exact shape (audit + a tool + a rule + a user-confirmed proposal), pointed at the rulebook instead of the efficiency-class units.
