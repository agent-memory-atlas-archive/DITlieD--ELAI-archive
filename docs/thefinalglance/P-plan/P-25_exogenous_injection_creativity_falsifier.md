## The bench — B-EXO-0 (single stage, real-fixture grain)

Design decision, on record: NO synthetic tier. Q-10 spent 3 runs and ~1,400 bridge calls on a closed-world synthetic tier that could not, by construction, reward external information. B-EXO-0 goes straight to the open-world real-fixture grain (the B-CREATIVITY-1 design), which is both the honest headroom test and the grain any adoption decision actually needs.

K=30 paired real ELAI improvement fixtures. Six arms in two preregistered families, same model/candidate cap (every completion becomes exactly 6 fixed `CandidateSlotV1` records, each holding a valid exo-card V2 or a closed pre-core invalid record)/token cap/parser/M3-frozen trusted in-process evaluator table, arm order seeded per task, generator never sees arm names. The prompt receives the exact hash-bound kind-specific `CandidateTaskViewV1` needed to construct a payload, while clauses/reference operations/reference machine/reference rows/metrics/expected outputs remain hidden. Every slot set binds that view and its sealed fixture. Arms 1-4 have one `SCORED_INITIAL` call/set; arms 5-6 have one `REVISION_SOURCE` call/set plus one `SCORED_REVISED` call/set:

1. DIRECT — target-only single self-coordinating call (the measured champion baseline).
2.
