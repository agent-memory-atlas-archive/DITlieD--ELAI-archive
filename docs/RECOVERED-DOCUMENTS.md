## Coverage and limits

The comparison covered Markdown/text and common documentation-support formats
across hidden and ignored source directories, with a second pass for alternative
document suffixes and a review of design media. Dependency environments, build
outputs and known downloaded source trees were pruned. Of the enumerated candidates,
3,440 paths were already public. The 31,151 missing candidates were accounted for:
303 are recovered here, 23 have equivalent original documents already represented
in the archive, and 30,825 remain deliberately excluded. The latter are mostly
copied dependencies, runtime records, worktree duplicates and raw experimental
data; they also include private correspondence, personal infrastructure, separate
projects and third-party screenshots. Private filenames are not listed here.

The counts describe this documentation audit, not a claim that every local file
belongs in the archive. Ten design-decision notes originally stored under an
excluded runtime directory are retained in [code-workflow-reasoning](recovered/code-workflow-reasoning/).
The rest retain their source-relative locations. The inventory records those relocations.

Personal names, local paths, private endpoints and contextual attribution were
generalized. The saved 335-finding design baseline retains its findings with
repository-relative file paths. Three ELAI concept boards retain their original
bytes and provenance metadata; external product screenshots are omitted, with
their [source index](../workflow/design/harness-references/INDEX.md) retained.

Every old instruction, measurement and completion claim is historical. A document
being restored does not establish that its proposal was implemented. Mockups use
sample state and have no working ELAI backend. Older links may point to omitted
local tools, private handoffs, raw runs or obsolete paths. Historical hashes and
line numbers may no longer match after privacy edits. See the [archive guide](../ARCHIVE.md).
