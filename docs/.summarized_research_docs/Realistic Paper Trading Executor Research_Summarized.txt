Floating-Point Precision Collapse:
- IEEE 754 cannot represent decimal fractions exactly
- $10.01 + 0.01 ≠ 10.02$ in floating-point arithmetic
- Microscopic errors accumulate into massive PnL deviations
- State logic failures: position == 0.0 checks fail due to artifacts
- Solution: Binary Coded Decimal, scaled integers (64-bit for hundred-millionths of cent)
