VRAM CALCULATION FORMULA:
VRAM = (P × B / 8) + (C × L × H × 2 / 1024)
Where P=parameters, B=bit-depth, C=context, L=layers, H=hidden_dim

QUANTIZATION DEPLOYMENT:

4-bit (GGUF Q4_K_M / AWQ):
- Best for edge deployment
- Highest compression, acceptable quality loss
- Foundation-Sec-8B: 5GB VRAM

6-bit (GGUF Q6_K / EXL2):
- Balance of compression and quality
- Preferred for production workloads
- Qwen 2.5 Coder 32B: 26GB VRAM

8-bit (GGUF Q8_0 / FP8):
- Maximum quality retention
- Requires substantial VRAM
- Llama 4 Maverick: 410GB VRAM
