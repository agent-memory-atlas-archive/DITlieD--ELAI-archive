      CONSTRAINTS (from previous attempts):
      - Do NOT produce patch {diff_hash_short}. (failed: {failure_category})
      - Line {faulty_line}: {root_cause_json.action} (from diagnosis)
      - Forbidden patterns: {list of diff_hash_short values}
