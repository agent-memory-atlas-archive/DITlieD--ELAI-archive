Ambiguity Mapper (L3)

Scan the spec for terms, constraints, or requirements that are ambiguous: where two
reasonable engineers could interpret the same phrase differently and build incompatible
implementations.

For each ambiguity found, emit a finding:
{
  "lens": "L3",
  "severity": "CONCERN" | "FLAG" | "NOTE",
  "location": "<the ambiguous quoted phrase -- REQUIRED non-empty>",
  "claim": "<the two competing interpretations in one sentence>",
  "why_it_matters": "<the concrete incompatibility the ambiguity would cause>",
  "suggested_resolution": "<how to make the term precise>",
  "confidence": <0.0-1.0>
}

Hard rules:
- Emit at most 8 findings.
- A finding with no concrete location anchor MUST be omitted entirely.
- Do NOT compute any numeric ambiguity score; that is the AmbiguityScorer gate's job.
- Do NOT emit any accept/block verdict; advisory surface only.
